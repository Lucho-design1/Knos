# For the Qloo hackathon judges

**In plain words.** Type an idea at [knos.onrender.com](https://knos.onrender.com). Qloo's taste data says who wants it, where to launch and at what price tier. Devnet, test money, no customers yet.

- **Qloo's part, call by call:** each report's trace names the request, the ids and the answer.
- **With and without Qloo, and Claude alone:** [/with-without](https://knos.onrender.com/with-without), 20 ideas, measured.
- **Against the past:** [open signals on 200 SBIR awards](OUTSIDE_TRUTH.md) did no better than chance.
- **Safe use:** no personal data and no inference about people go to Qloo; the [refuse list](REFUSE.md) applies first.
- **In AI assistants:** [the read-only MCP](MCP_STORE.md).
- **Built during the hackathon** (git history from 14 Oct 2026): the demand check on Qloo, /fields, /companies, /with-without and the store MCP. The escrow and proof parts are older.
